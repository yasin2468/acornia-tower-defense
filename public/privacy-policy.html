Acornia: Tower Defense — Privacy Policy

English — Privacy Policy

Last updated: 2026-11-23

Acornia: Tower Defense is an offline-first 3D woodland tower defense game that does not require an account.

  • No personal data collected: We do not collect, track, sell, or share names, emails, location data, advertising identifiers (IDFA/AAID), contacts, camera, or microphone data.
  • No third-party ad or analytics SDKs: No external ad networks, tracking pixels, or analytics SDKs run in this release.
  • Local device storage only: Stage progress, tower levels, and audio preferences are saved locally on your device. You can delete all local save data at any time from the Settings menu via Reset Save.
  • Contact: For any privacy questions, contact support@acorniagame.com.
