Command woodland guardians and defend the Sovereign Oak in Oakward: Tower Defense, a tactical offline 3D strategy game crafted for mobile portrait play.

Lead your hero directly on the battlefield, build elemental tower synergies, and hold the line against 14 relentless scrap-bot classes and towering mechanical bosses.

KEY FEATURES
• 50 Campaign Stages: Battle across 13 distinct biomes culminating in 12 build pads, 8 rune altars, and 14-wave boss sieges.
• 8 Towers, 16 Evolutions: Specialize Squirrel, Beaver, Owl, Hedgehog, Raccoon, Raven, Mole, and Ermine batteries into branching Tier-3 forms with live Synergy Combos.
• 4 Playable Heroes: Command Boz Pence, Crimson Tail, Rock Tusk, and Moon Wolf with manual movement, Viking/Royal stances, and high-impact ultimates.
• Deep Progression: Level unit cards to Lv.20, customize a 10-node Tactical Talent Tree, equip gear sets, and collect rewards from your Patrol Chest.
• 4 Challenge Modes: Test every build across Iron Mode, rotating Daily Mutator Challenges, Endless Siege waves, and the 5-stage Lost Forest Expedition.
• Private & Offline-First: No account required, zero ad SDKs, and portable PK1 save backup codes kept securely on your device.

Ready your towers and claim the Sovereign Oak Throne.
